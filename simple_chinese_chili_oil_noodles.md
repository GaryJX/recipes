# Simple Chinese Chili Oil Noodles

**Serves:** 1–2  
**Prep + cook time:** ~15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 6–8 oz (170–225 g) Chinese wheat noodles, fresh or dried
- 1–2 tbsp chili oil, preferably with chili flakes
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp Chinkiang (Chinese black) vinegar
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- 1–2 tbsp noodle cooking water
- 1 scallion, sliced
- Optional: 1 tsp sesame oil
- Optional: bok choy or other greens
- Optional: fried egg
- Optional: peanuts or sesame seeds
- Optional: ground pork

> **Sugar is optional.** You can skip it for a more savory, tangy flavor.

## Instructions

### 1. Make the sauce

In your serving bowl, mix together:

- Chili oil
- Soy sauce
- Black vinegar
- Minced garlic
- Sesame oil, if using

If you prefer a slightly sweeter flavor, add **½ tsp sugar**.

### 2. Cook the noodles

Cook the noodles according to the package directions.

Before draining, reserve **1–2 tbsp of the noodle cooking water**.

### 3. Combine

Add the hot noodles directly to the serving bowl.

Add the reserved noodle water and toss thoroughly until the sauce evenly coats the noodles.

### 4. Finish

Top with sliced scallions.

Add sesame seeds or peanuts if desired.

For a more substantial meal, serve with a **fried egg** and/or **bok choy**.

## Easy Flavor Ratio

A simple ratio to remember:

**2 parts chili oil : 2 parts soy sauce : 1 part black vinegar**

Then add garlic and adjust the chili oil to your preferred spice level.

### Adjust to taste

- **Too spicy:** Add more noodles, a little sesame oil, or a splash of noodle water.
- **Too salty:** Add more noodles or noodle water.
- **Too sharp/tangy:** Add a little more chili oil or a tiny amount of sugar.
- **Not spicy enough:** Add more chili oil or chili flakes.
